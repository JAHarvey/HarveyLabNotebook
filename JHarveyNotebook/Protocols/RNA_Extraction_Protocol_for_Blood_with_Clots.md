# RNA Isolation Protocol for Blood with Clots

*Harvey Lab | Modified on: May 11, 2026*

## Supplies

- Bucket of ice or cold beads
- Tri-reagent: 1 ml per sample + 1-Bromo-3-Chloropropane: 100 µl per sample
- Direct-zol RNA Miniprep Plus contents (Zymo Research, Cat# R2070 or R2071)
- Forceps and scissors (clean with RNase Away)
- 5 mm steel beads (clean with RNase Away, rinse with nuclease free water)
- 1 set of Safe-Lock 2 ml tubes, 2 sets of 1.7 ml tubes, 1 set of 0.5 ml tubes
- 100% Ethanol
- Access to a fume hood (Tri-reagent and 1-Bromo-3-Chloropropane handling)

## Disposal Guidelines

Tri-reagent, 1-Bromo-3-Chloropropane, and Directzol RNA Pre-wash are toxic and must not go in the trash or down the drain.

Pour all such waste into appropriate "biohazard container."

Dispose solid waste (tips, beads, tubes) in the red hazard waste bags.

## Pre-Start Checklist

- [ ] Clean work area and gather all supplies.
- [ ] Set centrifuge to 4° C.
- [ ] Clean beads, forceps, scissors, bench and fume hood with RNA Zap.
- [ ] Aliquot 1-Bromo-3-Chloropropane (100 µl per sample). Keep the aliquoted reagent and the outer metal part of the 2 ml Tissuelyser adaptor in the −80° C freezer.

## Step One: Sample Preparation

1. Pre-label and dispense cleaned steel beads and 1000 µl Trizol into 2 ml Safe-Lock tubes(A). Keep the samples in the ice bead bucket before ready to prevent RNA degradation.
2. After thawing, use a new set of forcep and scissor to cut the blood clot and dispense around 200 µl of the clot into each Safe-Lock tube. Vortex to mix for 30 seconds immediately after adding the blood.
3. Place safe lock tubes on the incubator, mix at 4 °C for 30 min.
4. Homogenize using TissuelyserII at 20 Hz, for 30 sec*6, with 30 sec pauses. Rotate tubes at the 4th interval inside out for consistency.

## Step Two: Phase Separation

1. Add 100 µl cold 1-Bromo-3-Chloropropane.
2. Vortex for 20 seconds, then incubate at room temperature for 10-15 minutes.
3. Centrifuge at 4°C for 60 minutes at 12,000 x g (Program 1).
   - Check phase separation: 50-60% of the volume should be clear aqueous phase. If separation is poor, add an additional 500 µl chloroform and repeat shake-incubate-spin step. Centrifugation separates the mixture into 3 phases: a colorless upper aqueous phase containing total RNA, an interphase containing DNA, and the red organic phase containing proteins.
4. Label a set of 1.7ml tubes (B) for collecting the aqueous phase.
5. Carefully collect up to 1000 µl of the aqueous phase, making sure not to disturb interphase or lower organic phase, and place in clean tube(B).

## Step Three: RNA Purification

While waiting:

- Label 1 new set of 1.7ml tubes (C) for elution.
- Label 1 set of 0.5ml tubes for quantification aliquot (D).

1. Add an equal volume (around 500 µl) of 100% Ethanol to the aqueous phase collected.
2. Transfer the mixture into a Zymo-Spin IIICG Column in a collection tube and centrifuge 12,000 x g for 30 seconds. Transfer the column into a new collection tube and discard the flow-through.
3. Add 400 µl RNA Wash Buffer to the column and centrifuge 12,000 x g for 30 seconds.
4. Add the master mix prepared directly to the column matrix. Incubate at room temperature (20 − 30° C) for 15 minutes. While waiting, discard the flow-through.
5. Add 400 µl Direct-zol RNA PreWash to the column and centrifuge 12,000 x g for 30 seconds. Discard the flow-through and repeat this step.
6. Add 700 µl RNA Wash Buffer to the column and centrifuge 12,000 x g for 2 minutes to ensure complete removal of the wash buffer. Transfer the column carefully into an RNase-free tube (C).
7. To elute RNA, add 50 µl of DNase/RNase-Free Water directly to the column matrix and centrifuge 12,000 x g for 30 seconds. Pipette the elusion buffer flow-through back into the column to repeat the step for higher yield.
8. Aliquot 2.5 µl of RNA for quantitation into 0.5ml tubes(D). Use 1 µl for Qubit, 1 µl for Bioanalyzer, with the remaining 0.5 µl to account for pipetting error. Store RNA in -80°C.
