# RNA Isolation Protocol for Lysed Blood (Challenge Study)

*Harvey Lab | Modified on: September 1, 2026*

## Supplies

- Bucket of ice or cold beads
- Trizol: extra 250 µl per sample + Chloroform: 200 µl per sample
- Direct-zol RNA Miniprep Plus contents (Zymo Research, Cat# R2070 or R2071)
- 2ml Sure-Lock tubes
- 100% Ethanol
- Access to a fume hood (Trizol and Chloroform handling)

## Sample Composition

The samples have been deactivated and homogenized.

Tube A contains: ∼220 µl DNA/RNA shield+ ∼25 µl of blood sample+ 750 µl Trizol

Tube B contains: ∼130 µl DNA/RNA shield+ ∼15 µl of blood sample+ 750 µl Trizol

## Disposal Guidelines

Trizol, Chloroform, and Directzol RNA Pre-wash are toxic and must not go in the trash or down the drain.

Pour all such waste into appropriate "biohazard container."

Dispose solid waste (tips, beads, tubes) in the red hazard waste bags.

## Pre-Start Checklist

- [ ] Clean work area and gather all supplies.
- [ ] Set centrifuge to 4° C.
- [ ] Wipe tools and fume hood with RNA Zap or similar product.
- [ ] Aliquot chloroform (200 µl per sample) 1 hr or the night before extraction. Keep the aliquoted reagent in the −80° C freezer.
- [ ] Keep the samples cold in the beads until ready to process.
- [ ] Warm RNase-free water or elution buffer to 60–70°C.
- [ ] Tube Label: St Jude 2026 Mall ID Tube ID Date

## Step One: Phase Separation

1. Prepare Surelock tubes with 250µl Trizol and label the tubes. Move all the sample into the Surelock tube.
2. Add 200 µl cold chloroform. Hand shake (eight figure 8's, DO NOT VORTEX), then incubate at room temperature for 10-15 minutes.
3. Centrifuge at 4°C for 60 minutes at max speed (Program 2) .
   - Check phase separation: 50-60% of the volume should be clear aqueous phase. If separation is poor, add an additional 200 µl chloroform and repeat shake-incubate-spin step. Centrifugation separates the mixture into 3 phases: a colorless upper aqueous phase containing total RNA, an interphase containing DNA, and the red organic phase containing proteins.
4. Carefully collect up to 400 µl, leave the rest to prevent DNA contamination of the aqueous phase into a new set of 1.7ml tubes, making sure not to disturb interphase or lower organic phase, and place in clean tube(B).

## Step Two: RNA Purification

While waiting:

- Make a master mix in an RNase-free tube, add 5 µl DNase I (6 U/µl)*, 75 µl DNA Digestion Buffer per sample and mix.

1. Add an equal volume (around 400 µl) of 100% Ethanol to the aqueous phase collected.
2. Transfer the mixture into a Zymo-Spin IIICG Column in a collection tube and centrifuge 12 x g for 30 seconds. Transfer the column into a new collection tube and discard the flow-through.
3. Add 400 µl RNA Wash Buffer to the column and centrifuge 12,000 x g for 30 seconds.
4. Add the master mix prepared directly to the column matrix. Incubate at room temperature (20 − 30° C) for 15 minutes. While waiting, discard the flow-through.
5. Add 400 µl Direct-zol RNA PreWash to the column and centrifuge 12,000 x g for 30 seconds. Discard the flow-through and repeat this step.
6. Add 700 µl RNA Wash Buffer to the column and centrifuge 12,000 x g for 2 minutes to ensure complete removal of the wash buffer. Transfer the column carefully into an RNase-free tube (C).
7. To elute RNA, add 50 µl of warm DNase/RNase-Free Water directly to the column matrix. Let it sit for 2–5 minutes.
8. Centrifuge the filter in a new 1.7 tube at 12,000 x g for 30 seconds. Add another 50 µl of warm DNase/RNase-Free Water directly to the column matrix, repeat the previous set, and collect the eluted solution in the same tube. Mix well.
9. Aliquot 2.5 µl of RNA from the elision for quantitation into 0.5ml tubes. Use 1 µl for Qubit, 1 µl for Bioanalyzer, with the remaining 0.5 µl to account for pipetting error. Split the rest of the elusion into two tubes. Tube A (for sequencing) should be 50 µl, tube B (backup) 47.5µl. Store RNA in -80°C.
