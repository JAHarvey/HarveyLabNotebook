# Whole Blood DNA Extraction Protocol (Zymo Quick DNA Kit)

*Harvey Lab | Modified on: March 5, 2026*

## Supplies

- Zymo Quick-DNA Miniprep Plus Kit (Cat# D4069), Proteinase K in the -20°C
- Kimtech Wipes
- Liquid Disposal Container
- 2 sets of 1.7ml sterile tubes
- 200 µl and 1000 µL pipettes and tips. Widebore tips when they arrive.

## Disposal Guidelines

1. Dispose tips and tubes that are in direct contact with blood into the red hazardous solid waste bag.
2. Dispose other tips and tubes in regular waste cup.
3. Pour all liquid waste into liquid disposal container.

## Pre-Start Checklist

- [ ] Clean work area with bleach and gather all supplies from the Zymo Kit.
- [ ] Take samples and reagent from freezer to thaw.

## Sample Processing

1. When the samples are completely thawed, centrifuge the samples for 30 seconds at 8,000 x g to to clear the tube walls and lids. ☐
2. Label one set of 1.7 ml tubes(A)and dispense half of the volume in each sample tube (∼300µl). ☐
3. Add 15 µl of Proteinase K to 300 µl of the sample/shield mixture. ☐
4. Vortex 10-15 seconds and then incubate the tube at 55°C 800 rpm in the Thermo Shaker for 20 minutes. ☐

   While waiting:

   - Label 1 new set of 1.7ml tubes (B) for elution.
   - Label 1 set of Zymo-Spin™ IIC-XLR Columns for DNA collection.
   - Label 2 sets of collection tubes for waste flow through.

5. Add 1 volume (∼315 µl) Genomic Binding Buffer to the digested sample. Vortex 10-15 seconds. ☐
6. Transfer the mixture to a Zymo-Spin™ IIC-XLR Column in a Collection Tube. Centrifuge at 12,000 x g for 1 minute. Discard the flow through and collection tube. ☐
7. Add 400 µl DNA Pre-Wash Buffer to the spin column in a new Collection Tube. Centrifuge at 12,000 x g for 1 minute. Empty the Collection Tube. Wipe off the edge on Kimtech wipes to prevent dripping every time. ☐
8. Add 700 µl g-DNA Wash Buffer to the spin column. Centrifuge at 12,000 x g for 1 minute. Empty the Collection Tube. ☐
9. Add 200 µl g-DNA Wash Buffer to the spin column. Centrifuge at 12,000 x g for 1 minute. Discard the Collection Tube with the flow through. ☐
10. Transfer the spin column to a clean microcentrifuge tube. Add 50 µl DNA Elution Buffer directly on the matrix. Incubate for 5 minutes at room temperature, then centrifuge at maximum speed for 1 minute to elute the DNA. ☐
11. Pipette the flow through back into the column, centrifuge at 12,000 x g for 1 minute to increase yield. ☐
12. Store all samples in the -80°C for future use. ☐
