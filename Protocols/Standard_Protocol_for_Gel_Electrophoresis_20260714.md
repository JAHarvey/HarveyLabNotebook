# Gel Electrophoresis Protocol

*Harvey Lab | Modified on: July 14, 2026*

## Supplies

- Agarose Powder: 1 g (for 100 ml 1% gel)
- TBE buffer for gel (fresh from carboy): 100 ml (for 100 ml 1% gel)
- Sample DNA
- Loading Dye and Ladder from −20° C freezer.
- Gel Green 10000X
- Parafilm sheet
- TBE buffer for gel tank (reuse in blue cap bottle)
- Gel Tank
- Microwave

## Pre-Start Checklist

- [ ] Clean work area with bleach and gather all supplies.

## Step One: Gel Casting

1. Measure 1g of agarose powder with spatula, weigh boat, and scale. Measure 100 ml of TBE buffer from the carboy by the sink.
2. Mix agarose powder with 100 mL 1xTAE in a microwavable flask. (If reusing gel, start from bullet 3.)
3. Label the flask with tape, weigh the flask with the tape on it, and write down the total mass on the tape. We record this to add nuclease free water back to the tube to make the mass of the gel even after water evaporation.
4. Microwave for 1-2 min until the agarose is completely dissolved. (Swirl the bottle with silicone pinch matt after 1 min, if the solution does not look clear, with dusty suspension, add 30 sec to 1 min.) Be careful stirring, eruptive boiling can occur.
5. Weigh the flask again when the agarose powder look completely dissolved. Add enough DI water to the flask until it reach the labeled mass. This step helps cooling down the gel a little and make sure the concentration is correct.
6. Let agarose solution cool down for 2 min to about 50 °C when you can touch it with your hand.
7. Add 10 µl of Gel Green 10000X to the flask and swirl to mix.
8. Pour the gel mixture into the cask with combs inserted.
9. Using a pipette tip, move any bubbles to the side of the tray
10. Wait for 20 min until the gel solidifies completely. Once solidified, turn the gel into the direction, with thet wells closer to the top end with the black negative wire.

## Step Two: Sample Loading and Running the Gel

1. Fill gel box with 1X TBE until the gel is covered.
2. Write down the arrangement of each sample in the gel plate.
3. A) - If you're using clear buffer during PCR: make a big mix of loading dye and DI water for all your ladders and samples.
   - Label a 1.7 ml tube as a mixing tube. Each well should be loaded with 3 µl PCR product, 1 µl of loading dye, and 2 µl of DI water. Make the master mix of DI water and loading dye according to sample number. (e.g. For 18 wells, add 18 µl of loading dye, 36 µl of DI water.)
   - Load the first well in each row with ladder. The ladder is a very concentrated solution with different sizes of DNA, so we won't need a large volume of it. Mix 1 µl of ladder with 2 µl of the loading dye/DI water mix is good enough.
   - The rest of the wells should be loaded with 3 µl PCR product, 1 µl of loading dye, and 2 µl of DI water. Make the master mix of DI water and loading dye according to sample number. (for 16 wells, add 16 µl of loading dye, 48 µl of DI water.
   - Pipette 3 µl drops of the mix on the parafilm sheet. Add 3 µl of each PCR product into the liquid drops, keep the same tip, pipette in and out a few times to mix, then load the entire drop into the well.
   - Complete this step for all samples.

   B) - If you're using the Green buffer for PCR, no loading dye is needed for each DNA sample. Make the loading dye/DI water mix for the ladder only. So for one ladder in each of the two rows, you'll mix the 8 µl of DI water and 2 µl of the loading dye directly on the parafilm, and then add 2 µl of the ladder, and pipette 6 µl of the mixture into the first well of each row.
   - Load 3 µl of the Green PCR product into each well.
4. Run the gel at 100 V until the dye line is approximately 75-80% of the way down the gel. A typical run time is about 1 hour, depending on the gel concentration and voltage.

## Step Three: Imaging and Cleaning Up

1. Once done, take the tray out of the gel tank in gloves. Slide the gel into your hands. Bring over to the imager and set in the center of the lighting part.
2. Cover the lighting imager with the enclosure. Look through the opening. Update results in the datasheet.
3. Place the orange shield over the gel to take picture.
4. Pour the leftover TAE from the gel box into the used TAE container using a funnel.
5. Rinse the gel box, comb, and flask in DI water
6. Wipe the imager with a Kimwipe and DI water
7. To reuse gel, use the needle and cut the gel into strips, put them back into the flask, and seal using a parafilm. Each freshly made gel can be reused 3-4 times.
