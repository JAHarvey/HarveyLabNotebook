# PCR for Turkey Study

*Harvey Lab | Modified on: July 14, 2026*

## Supplies

- Flat ice brick
- Your own set of PCR reagents to prevent contamination: nuclease-free water, 5X GoTaq Flexi buffer, MgCl2, dNTP, Primer (forward and reverse), GoTaq DNA polymerase (-20° C freezer)
- strip tubes, 1.7mL tubes
- Template DNA (-80° C freezer)
- PCR thermocycler

## Pre-Start Checklist

- [ ] Clean work area with bleach.
- [ ] Vortex and spin down all reagents before usage.
- [ ] Keep DNA polymerase in the freezer as long as possible. Start thawing this tube only when you're ready.
- [ ] Keep as much as reagents, master mix cold on the ice brick to prevent degradation and primer dimer.

## Step One: Prepare Master Mix (GAPDH)

The volume is for 16 reactions for the control gene GAPDH, but we are processing 14 samples only: 1 for pipetting error, 1 for negative control.

1. Add 14.375 µl of nuclease-free water into each well of the strip tube.
2. Label a 1.7 ml tube master mix.
3. Add 80 µl of 5x GoTaq Flexi Buffer.
4. Add 32 µl of MgCl2.
5. Add 8 µl of dNTP.
6. Add 8 µl of forward primer.
7. Add 8 µl of reverse primer.
8. Add 2 µl of GoTaq DNA Polymerase.
9. Vortex and spin down the master mix, aliquot 8.625 µl of the mix into each well, it should be enough for 15 wells.

## Step Two: Loading DNA and running Thermocycler

1. Add 2µl of DNA template into each well. When we're moving on to testing LPDV and REV, always include a positive control as well.
2. Load 2 µl of nuclease-free water into the last well. Use that well as the negative control.
3. Load the strip tube with lid on into the Thermocycler. Turn the power on, and start running the protocol of the gene your working on.
4. Once the program is finished, the machine will store it at 4° C. Pause the program, take the sample out and store it in the 4° C fridge, if you're not doing gel immediately.
