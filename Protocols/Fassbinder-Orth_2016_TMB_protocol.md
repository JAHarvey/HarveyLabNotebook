# IgY ELISA Protocol — TMB Substrate Method

*Sep 22, 2026*

## Materials & Reagents

- Nunc Maxisorp C-bottom 96-well plates
- Coating buffer: Dissolve each Carbonate-Bicarbonate capsule (C3041) in 100mL Deionized water. Store in 50mL falcon tubes in 4 degree fridge.
- Wash buffer: 1X PBS with 0.05% Tween-20. Dilute each bag of PBS-Tw salt (P3563) in 1L of Deionized water. Store in 50mL falcon tubes in 4 degree fridge.
- Blocking buffer: To make 5% buffer, rehydrate each container of Non-fat Dried Milk bovine (M7409) with 60mL of 1XPBS with 0.05% Tween-20 solution.
- Secondary antibody (stored with glycerol in -20 degree freezer) goat anti-chicken IgY-HRP, diluted 1:10,000 in blocking buffer
- Substrate: TMB Substrate Solution, ready-to-use (N301)
- Stop solution: 0.016M sulfuric acid
- Positive controls: pooled bird serum, included on every plate for cross-plate standardization
- Equipment: Agilent plate washer; Agilent plate reader

## Day 1 — Coating

1. Dilute each serum sample 1:15,000 in coating buffer. (dilution ratio variable across species)
2. Add 75 µL of diluted sample per well, in triplicate. Cap at 24 samples per plate so triplicates plus controls fit within the 96 wells.
3. Include pooled waterfowl serum (positive control) on every plate.
4. Put in drawer, protect from light and incubate overnight at room temperature.(Min 16hrs)

## Day 2 — Blocking through Read

1. Remove the coating solution with plate washer. (program ASP)
2. Add 200 µL/well blocking buffer; incubate 60 min at room temperature.
3. Wash 4× with wash buffer. (program SLOW WASH)
4. Add 100 µL/well of the anti-IgY-HRP antibody, diluted 1:10,000 in blocking buffer.
5. Incubate 1 h at 37°C.
6. Wash 4× with wash buffer.
7. Add 100 µL/well TMB substrate.
8. Incubate at room temperature, in the dark, for 8 minutes.
9. Stop with 100 µL/well of sulfuric acid.
10. Read absorbance at 450 nm/630 nm.
11. Export Data into Excel File.
